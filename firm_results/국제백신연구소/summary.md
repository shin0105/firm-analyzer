# Summary: 국제백신연구소 (International Vaccine Institute, IVI)

## 1. What the Company Does
국제백신연구소 (IVI) is a non-profit organization focused on global health initiatives, particularly in vaccine research, development, and delivery. Key business areas include:
- **Vaccine development**: Creating vaccines for neglected and emerging infectious diseases.
- **Research**: Conducting laboratory studies, disease surveillance, and epidemiological research to understand disease patterns.
- **Clinical trials**: Testing vaccine efficacy and safety through trials.
- **Capacity building**: Supporting vaccine delivery systems in low- and middle-income countries to improve public health.
- **Public health initiatives**: Collaborating with international partners to advance health outcomes, especially for children in developing countries.

## 2. Research Areas and Technologies
IVI is involved in:
- **Neglected infectious diseases**: Researching diseases that disproportionately affect underserved populations.
- **Emerging infectious diseases**: Studying new and re-emerging health threats globally.
- **Vaccine efficacy**: Measuring the effectiveness of vaccines through clinical trials and disease monitoring.
- **Stakeholder engagement**: Conducting analyses to engage relevant stakeholders in public health strategies.
- **Molecular immunology**: Exploring immune responses at the molecular level to improve vaccine design and development.
