# 국립산림과학원 (National Institute of Forest Science)

## 1. What the Organization Does
The 국립산림과학원 (National Institute of Forest Science) is a leading forest research institution in South Korea. Its primary mission is to utilize forests to enhance the national brand and contribute to sustainable development. The organization focuses on various aspects of forest science, including:

- Promoting forest-related research and innovation.
- Supporting national strategies for forest management and conservation.
- Providing scientific and technical services related to forestry.

## 2. Research Areas and Technologies
The institution is involved in diverse research areas, organized into specialized departments:

- **Future Forest Strategy Research Division**: Focused on long-term strategies for sustainable forest management and policy development.
- **Forest Disaster and Environment Research Division**: Researching forest-related disasters (e.g., landslides) and environmental challenges.
- **Forest Biological Resources Research Division**: Exploring the biological resources within forests, including biodiversity conservation.
- **Wood Engineering Research Division**: Specialized in wood science and engineering, including the development of wood-based materials and technologies.

The organization also conducts research to address climate change, enhance forest productivity, and protect ecosystems.
