### Summary of 서울대학교병원의생명연구원 (Seoul National University Hospital Biomedical Research Institute)

#### 1. **What the company does (Business areas, products, services)**
서울대학교병원의생명연구원 (SNUH Biomedical Research Institute) is a leading research institution affiliated with Seoul National University Hospital. It focuses on advancing medical and biomedical research, providing support for clinical trials, and developing innovative healthcare solutions. Key areas include:
- Clinical trial management and support.
- Medical research collaboration through specialized centers, such as the Medical Research Cooperation Center.
- Hosting workshops, seminars, and symposiums to promote research and knowledge exchange.
- Utilizing healthcare big data and artificial intelligence (AI) for medical advancements.

#### 2. **Research areas and technologies**
The institute is involved in cutting-edge research in the following areas:
- **Molecular Biology**: Exploring molecular mechanisms for disease understanding and treatment.
- **Orthopedics**: Developing new treatments for orthopedic conditions.
- **Cardiovascular and Chronic Disease Therapy**: Innovating therapies for heart-related and chronic illnesses.
- **Pediatric and Adolescent Psychiatry**: Advancing mental health research for younger populations.
- **Neuroengineering**: Combining engineering and neuroscience for medical applications.
- **Data Medicine and Bioinformatics**: Leveraging big data and AI for precision medicine.
- **Cancer Research**: Focused on lymphoma, leukemia, and kidney cancer.
- **Smart Clinical Trials**: Building platforms for future-oriented clinical trials.
