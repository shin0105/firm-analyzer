# Summary of '한국과학기술원경영대학' (KAIST College of Business)

## 1. What the Organization Does
KAIST College of Business (한국과학기술원경영대학) is part of KAIST, a leading research university in South Korea. The institution focuses on:
- **Education**: Training future leaders in science, technology, and business through programs such as the Executive MBA and other management-focused degrees.
- **Research**: Conducting applied and fundamental research to support national policies, long-term R&D projects, and collaborations with industry.
- **Industry Collaboration**: Bridging academia and industry by integrating research outcomes into practical applications.

## 2. Research Areas and Technologies
The organization is involved in:
- **Big Data and Artificial Intelligence (AI)**: Research in advanced data analytics and AI technologies.
- **Production and Strategy**: Studies in production management and strategic business planning.
- **Technology Management**: Research on the management of emerging technologies and innovation.
- **Policy-Oriented Research**: Supporting national development through applied research in science and technology.

## 3. Recruitment Information
- **Open Positions**: 
  - Positions for faculty in fields like computer science, big data, AI, and web development.
  - Administrative roles in research and general operations.
  - Specific recruitment for Executive MBA students and other specialized programs.
- **Job Types**: Opportunities include full-time faculty roles (PhD required), administrative positions, and internships.
- **Application Process**:
  - Recruitment information is regularly updated on the career development center's website.
  - Interested candidates can contact the career development office via email (career@kaist.ac.kr) for more details.

## Additional Information
- KAIST College of Business actively connects students and alumni with industry opportunities through its career development center, which posts internship and job openings year-round.
