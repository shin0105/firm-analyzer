# Summary of '한국과학기술원 부설 고등과학원 (KIAS)'

## 1. What the Company Does
- **Core Mission**: The Korea Institute for Advanced Study (KIAS) was established in 1996 as a government-funded research institute under the Ministry of Science and ICT. Its primary goal is to advance Korea's basic sciences to a global level.
- **Focus**: KIAS is dedicated to pure theoretical basic science research, enabling researchers to focus solely on their studies without administrative burdens. It aims to foster future-oriented scientific talent and lead advancements in basic science.
- **Structure**: The institute operates three main academic divisions:
  - **Mathematics Division**: Covers areas like algebra, number theory, geometry, topology, partial differential equations, and applied mathematics.
  - **Physics Division**: Engages in theoretical physics, including string theory, quantum field theory, particle physics, statistical physics, condensed matter physics, astrophysics, and cosmology.
  - **Computational Sciences Division**: Focuses on computational approaches to fundamental problems in science.

## 2. Research Areas and Technologies
- **Mathematics**: Research spans pure and applied mathematics, including algebraic geometry, mirror symmetry, and topological studies. Renowned scholars like Fields Medalists June Huh and Efim Zelmanov are part of the faculty.
- **Physics**: Studies include advanced topics like string theory, quantum mechanics, and cosmology.
- **AI and Computational Sciences**: The AI Basic Science Center conducts interdisciplinary research in artificial intelligence, integrating math, physics, and computational science.
- **Educational Initiatives**: KIAS also organizes summer camps and programs to inspire and educate students in basic sciences.
